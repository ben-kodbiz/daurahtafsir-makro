About & Sources

Purpose

This site provides free access to recorded lessons and tafsir notes from the Daurah Tafsir Makro study programme — a structured study of the tafsir of the Quran and hadith, covering all 114 surahs.

Teacher / Source

All lessons are delivered by Maulana Asri of Darul Kautsar. The tafsir notes (PDF) and video recordings are the work of the teacher and this programme.

Content Rights

The lesson recordings, PDF notes, and other study material are the property of their author and are shared here for educational purposes. This website does not claim ownership of the teaching material itself.
